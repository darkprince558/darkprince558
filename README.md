# Anish Jami

**I build software for robots and connected systems.**

I study Mechatronics Systems Engineering and AI Systems Engineering at Western University. I’m interested in how machines share information, what happens when they lose contact, and how people figure out what went wrong.

[Portfolio](https://anish-jami.vercel.app/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anish-jami/)

## What I’m building

**[ECITON-R](https://ecitonrobotics.com/)** · Robot swarm research\
I’m studying how robots can share discoveries and divide work when their network is unreliable. In [Swarm Lab](https://ecitonrobotics.com/swarm-lab), you can follow an individual simulated robot and see how a message changes what it knows and does.

**[Dispatch Robotics](https://dispatch-robotics.vercel.app/)** · Warehouse robotics operations\
I started Dispatch to give warehouse teams one place to see fleet activity, facility maps, and incidents across robot vendors. The [Dispatch Observe demo](https://dispatch-robotics.vercel.app/demo) shows how an operator can trace an incident back to the events behind it.
